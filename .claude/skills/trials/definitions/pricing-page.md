## Brief

Design a pricing page for a note-taking app called Marginalia. Three tiers — Free, Pro ($8/mo), Team ($15/user/mo) — with Pro highlighted as the recommended plan. Each tier lists 4–5 features, has a call-to-action button, and there's a short FAQ of three questions under the tiers. Desktop width, light theme. Build it in the Figma file I have open, on a new page named "{page}".

## Rubric

- Tier cards read as one system: consistent padding, type scale, and button treatment.
- Pro is visibly recommended without shouting (contrast, position, or badge — not all three).
- Feature lists align across cards; the FAQ has real hierarchy, not three bold lines.
- Would survive a real launch page with no cleanup.
