## Brief

Design a three-step onboarding flow for a mobile budgeting app (390×844). Step 1 asks for a monthly income with a big numeric input; step 2 picks spending categories from a grid of eight chips; step 3 is a summary with a "Start budgeting" button. Each screen has a progress indicator, a back control, and a skip link. Friendly, warm, light theme. Put all three screens side by side in the Figma file I have open, on a new page named "{page}".

## Rubric

- The three screens share one visual system — progress indicator, buttons, and type identical across them.
- Step 2's chip grid wraps and aligns; selected and unselected states are both present.
- The numeric input on step 1 reads as an input, with a keyboard-appropriate affordance.
- Warmth comes from palette and type choices, not from decoration.
