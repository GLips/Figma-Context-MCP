## Brief

Design the hero section of a marketing site for a developer tool that turns Figma designs into code. Headline, one-sentence subhead, primary and secondary buttons, a row of five customer logos (use plain wordmarks), and a product screenshot placeholder framed at an angle to the right. 1440 wide. Bold, confident, dark theme with one accent colour. Build it in the Figma file I have open, on a new page named "{page}".

## Rubric

- Headline sets the hierarchy; subhead and buttons fall in behind it with clear steps in size and weight.
- The angled screenshot frame is a real transform with a believable shadow, not a skewed rectangle.
- The logo row is evenly spaced and optically balanced.
- The single accent is used sparingly and lands on the primary action.
