## Brief

Design an analytics dashboard for a small SaaS. Left sidebar with the product name and five nav items; main area with a header row (page title, date-range control, primary button) and a responsive grid of six stat cards — each with a label, a big number, a delta chip (up or down), and a small sparkline. One wider card at the bottom holds a bar chart of the last 12 months. Desktop, 1440 wide, light theme. Build it in the Figma file I have open, on a new page named "{page}".

## Rubric

- The grid is an actual grid: equal gutters, cards fill their tracks, the wide card spans cleanly.
- Sparklines and the bar chart are drawn data, not rectangles labelled "chart".
- Delta chips carry the up/down semantics in colour and glyph.
- Sidebar and header feel like the same product as the cards.
