## Brief

Design a mobile music player "now playing" screen (390×844). Album art dominates the top two thirds, then track title and artist, a scrubber with elapsed and remaining time, and a transport row: shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat. Dark theme with a tint pulled from the album art. Build it in the Figma file I have open, on a new page named "{page}".

## Rubric

- Album art, scrubber, and transport row are real UI — proportioned and aligned — not a gradient-and-text mockup.
- Transport icons are drawn shapes, consistent in weight and optical size.
- The dark palette has depth: surface, elevated surface, and accent are distinguishable.
- The screen would pass as a shipped app in a screenshot.
